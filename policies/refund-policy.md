# Returns & Refunds

Changed your mind? It happens. Here's how to send it back without the drama.

You have **14 days** from the day your order arrives to tell us you want to
return it, and another **14 days** after that to ship it back. This is your right
under EU consumer law — we're not doing you a favour, it's just how we run.

## What can come back

To get a full refund, the piece needs to return the way it left:

- Unworn, unwashed, and undamaged
- With all original tags still attached
- In its original packaging where possible

We can't accept anything worn, washed, altered, or that comes back smelling like
a night out.

## How to start a return

1. Email **supernovaoficial066@gmail.com** with your order number and which item(s) you want to
   return — **don't ship anything before you hear back from us.**
2. Because our items ship from our fulfilment partners, we'll send you the
   correct return address and instructions for your specific order.
3. Pack it up and ship it. **Return shipping is on you** unless the item was
   faulty or we sent the wrong thing (see below).

We recommend a tracked service — until the parcel reaches the return address,
it's your responsibility, and we can't refund what's never received.

## Refunds

Once your return arrives and passes a quick check, we'll refund you within
**14 days** to your original payment method, and email you when it's done.
Depending on your bank, it can take a few extra days to appear.

The refund covers the price of the item(s). Original shipping costs, and any
customs/import charges you paid, aren't refundable unless the return is our
fault.

## Wrong or faulty item?

If something arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered, that's on us
— not you. Email **supernovaoficial066@gmail.com** within **14 days** of delivery with a photo
and your order number. We'll cover the return and sort a replacement or full
refund, your call.

## Exchanges

The fastest way to swap a size or colour is to return the original for a refund
and place a new order. Stock moves fast — we can't hold pieces.

## Still stuck?

Reach us at **supernovaoficial066@gmail.com** and a human will get back to you.

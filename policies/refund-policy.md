# Returns & Refunds

Changed your mind? It happens. Here's how to send it back without the drama.

You have **14 days** from the day your order lands to tell us you want to return
it, and another **14 days** after that to actually ship it back. This is your
right under EU consumer law — we're not doing you a favour, it's just how we run.

## What can come back

To get a full refund, the piece needs to return the way it left:

- Unworn, unwashed, and undamaged
- With all original tags still attached
- In its original packaging where possible

We can't accept anything that's been worn, washed, altered, or that comes back
smelling like a night out. For hygiene reasons, **[underwear / swimwear — remove
this line if you don't sell them]** can't be returned once the seal is opened.

## How to start a return

1. Email us at **[RETURNS EMAIL]** with your order number and which item(s) you
   want to send back.
2. We'll reply with the return address and instructions.
3. Pack it up and ship it. **Return shipping is on you** unless the item was
   faulty or we sent the wrong thing (see below).

We recommend a tracked service — until the parcel reaches us, it's your
responsibility, and we can't refund what we never receive.

## Refunds

Once your return arrives and passes a quick check, we'll refund you within
**14 days** to your original payment method. We'll email you when it's done.
Depending on your bank, it can take a few extra days to show up.

The refund covers the price of the item(s). Original shipping costs aren't
refunded unless the return is our fault.

## Wrong or faulty item?

If something arrives damaged, defective, or just isn't what you ordered, that's
on us — not you. Email **[RETURNS EMAIL]** within **14 days** of delivery with a
photo and your order number. We'll cover the return shipping and sort out a
replacement or full refund, your call.

## Exchanges

The fastest way to swap a size or colour is to return the original for a refund
and place a new order. Stock moves fast around here — we can't hold pieces.

## Still stuck?

Reach us at **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** and a human will get back to you.

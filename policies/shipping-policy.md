# Shipping

We pack fast and ship straight. Here's what to expect.

## Where we ship

Right now SUPERNOVA ships **within the Netherlands only**. We're a glitch that's
still spreading — international shipping across the EU and beyond is coming soon.
Want us in your country? Tell us at **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** and you'll move us up the
list.

## Processing time

Orders are packed within **[ORDER PROCESSING TIME, e.g. 1–2 business days]**
(Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays). Orders placed on weekends or
holidays go out the next business day. You'll get a confirmation email with
tracking the moment your parcel leaves us.

## Delivery times & cost (Netherlands)

| Method | Estimated delivery | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard ([CARRIER, e.g. PostNL]) | 1–3 business days | **[STANDARD SHIPPING COST]** |
| Free shipping | 1–3 business days | **Free on orders over €50** |

Delivery estimates are guidelines, not guarantees — once a parcel is with the
carrier, the timing is in their hands.

## Tracking

Every order ships tracked. Use the link in your shipping confirmation email to
follow it. If tracking hasn't moved in a few days, give it a little time, then
hit us up at **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

## Lost, delayed, or damaged parcels

- **Delayed?** Carriers occasionally run slow, especially around drops and
  holidays. If it's significantly past the estimate, contact us and we'll chase
  it with the carrier.
- **Marked delivered but not there?** Check with neighbours and around your
  building first, then email us — we'll help track it down.
- **Arrived damaged?** Email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** within **14 days** with photos
  and your order number and we'll make it right.

## Wrong address

Double-check your shipping details at checkout. We can't reroute a parcel once
it's shipped, and orders returned to us due to an incorrect address may incur a
re-shipping cost.

## International (coming soon)

As we expand, EU and international rates, delivery windows, and any customs or
import duties will be listed here. For deliveries outside the EU, import duties
and taxes are the customer's responsibility.

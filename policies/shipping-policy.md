# Shipping

We pack fast and ship straight. Here's what to expect — read this so there are
no surprises.

## Where we ship

Right now SUPERNOVA ships **to the Netherlands**. We're a glitch that's still
spreading — more of the EU and beyond is coming soon. Want us in your country?
Tell us at **supernovaoficial066@gmail.com**.

## How our shipping works

Our pieces are shipped directly from our production and fulfilment partners. That
keeps prices sharp, but it also means delivery takes longer than a local webshop
— this isn't next-day stuff, and that's the honest truth.

## Processing & delivery times

- **Order processing:** [1–3 business days] to confirm and prepare your order.
- **Estimated delivery:** **[7–20 business days]** after processing, depending on
  the supplier and customs.

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. Once a parcel is with the carrier,
timing is out of our hands. Drops and holiday periods can add a few days.

## Shipping cost

- **Free shipping on orders over €50.**
- Orders under €50: **[STANDARD SHIPPING COST]**.

## Customs, import VAT & duties

Because orders may ship from outside the EU, your parcel can be subject to
**import VAT and/or customs duties** charged by your local authorities on
delivery. **These charges are the customer's responsibility** and are not
included in the price you pay at checkout. They depend on your country's rules
and the order value — we have no control over them.

> We're working on smoother EU-based fulfilment to reduce delays and customs. As
> that rolls out, this page will be updated.

## Tracking

Where tracking is available, you'll get a link by email once your order ships. If
tracking hasn't updated in a while, give it some time, then reach us at
**supernovaoficial066@gmail.com**.

## Lost, delayed, or damaged parcels

- **Delayed?** International shipping runs slow sometimes. If it's well past the
  estimate, contact us and we'll chase it with the supplier/carrier.
- **Marked delivered but not there?** Check around your address and with
  neighbours first, then email us.
- **Arrived damaged?** Email **supernovaoficial066@gmail.com** within **14 days** with photos
  and your order number and we'll make it right.

## Wrong address

Double-check your details at checkout. We can't reroute a parcel once it ships,
and orders returned due to an incorrect address may incur a re-shipping cost.

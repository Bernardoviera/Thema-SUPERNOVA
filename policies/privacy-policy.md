## What we collect

- **Order info:** name, shipping/billing address, email, phone number.
- **Payment info:** processed securely by our payment providers — we never see
  or store your full card details.
- **Account info** (if you create an account): email and a password.
- **Communications:** messages you send us by email or contact form.
- **Technical & usage data:** IP address, device/browser type, and how you use
  the site, collected through cookies and similar tech (see Cookies below).

## Why we use it, and our legal basis

- **To fulfil your order** — process payment, ship, and provide support.
  *Legal basis: performance of a contract.*
- **To run and secure the store** — prevent fraud, fix bugs, improve the site.
  *Legal basis: our legitimate interests.*
- **To send marketing** (drops, offers) — only if you've opted in. You can
  unsubscribe anytime. *Legal basis: your consent.*
- **To meet legal obligations** — e.g. tax and accounting records.
  *Legal basis: legal obligation.*

## Who we share it with

We only share what's needed to run the store, with processors bound to protect
your data:

- **Shopify** — our e-commerce platform (hosting, checkout, order management).
- **Payment providers** — to process payments securely.
- **Shipping carriers** — e.g. [CARRIER] — to deliver your order.
- **Email/marketing tools** — to send order updates and, with consent, marketing.

We never sell your personal data. Some providers may process data outside the
EU; where they do, appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses)
are in place.

## How long we keep it

We keep order and transaction data for as long as needed to provide the service
and to meet legal/tax obligations (in the Netherlands, typically **7 years** for
financial records). Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to
delete it.

## Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart working, remember preferences, and understand
how the site is used. You can manage or block cookies in your browser settings,
though some features (like checkout) need essential cookies to work.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

- Access the data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Ask us to delete it ("right to be forgotten")
- Restrict or object to how we use it
- Receive your data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these, email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**. We'll respond within one
month.

## Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your data, please contact us first so we can fix
it. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority,
the **Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens** (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

Your data isn't the product here — your fit is. This policy explains what we
collect, why, and the rights you have over it. We follow the EU General Data
Protection Regulation (GDPR) for our customers in Europe, and Brazil's LGPD.

## What we collect

- **Order info:** name, shipping/billing address, email, phone number.
- **Payment info:** processed securely by our payment providers — we never see or
  store your full card details.
- **Account info** (if you create an account): email and a password.
- **Communications:** messages you send us by email or contact form.
- **Technical & usage data:** IP address, device/browser type, and how you use
  the site, collected through cookies and similar tech (see Cookies below).

## Why we use it, and our legal basis

- **To fulfil your order** — process payment, ship via our partners, and provide
  support. *Legal basis: performance of a contract.*
- **To run and secure the store** — prevent fraud, fix bugs, improve the site.
  *Legal basis: our legitimate interests.*
- **To send marketing** (drops, offers) — only if you've opted in. Unsubscribe
  anytime. *Legal basis: your consent.*
- **To meet legal obligations** — e.g. tax and accounting records. *Legal basis:
  legal obligation.*

## Who we share it with

We only share what's needed to run the store, with processors bound to protect
your data:

- **Shopify** — our e-commerce platform (hosting, checkout, order management).
- **Payment providers** — to process payments securely.
- **Fulfilment partners & shipping carriers** — to produce and deliver your
  order. To do this, your name and shipping address are shared with the supplier
  handling your order.
- **Email/marketing tools** — for order updates and, with consent, marketing.

We never sell your personal data. Because our suppliers and tools may operate
outside the EU and Brazil, your data may be processed internationally; where it
is, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep it

We keep order and transaction data for as long as needed to provide the service
and meet legal/tax obligations. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe or
ask us to delete it.

## Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart working, remember preferences, and understand
how the site is used. You can manage or block cookies in your browser settings,
though essential cookies are needed for things like checkout to work.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR in the EU, LGPD in Brazil), you can:

- Access the data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Ask us to delete it
- Restrict or object to how we use it
- Receive your data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these, email **supernovaoficial066@gmail.com**. We'll respond within the
time required by law (one month under the GDPR).

## Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your data, please contact us first so we can fix
it. EU customers also have the right to complain to their national Data
Protection Authority (in the Netherlands, the **Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens**).
In Brazil, the relevant authority is the **ANPD**.
